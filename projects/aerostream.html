Project Overview

Real-time meteorological wind shear prediction tool for commercial drone operations.

Project Details

AeroStream aggregates Doppler radar feeds and ground weather station telemetry to calculate safe flight corridors and automated landing envelopes for delivery drone fleets in metropolitan wind tunnels.
